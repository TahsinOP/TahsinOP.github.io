About

The journey so far: experience, education and teaching.

I started in mechanical design, building ring-throwing robots for Robocon, and moved steadily up the stack: arm motion planning, then embedded control and navigation, then SLAM and state estimation, and now multi-robot manipulation and learned policies. Most of what I build spans all three layers: the mechanism, the electronics and the software that runs on them.
