Decentralised Cooperative Mobile Manipulation

Researcher · IIT Bombay

Advised by Prof. Shivaram Kalyanakrishnan, Prof. Harshad Khadilkar, Prof. Nived Chebrolu

  • Cooperative transport of a rigid payload by two nonholonomic mobile manipulators, using onboard vision and decentralised compute only — no motion capture, no shared map and no force/torque sensing at the grasp.
  • Planning and control are posed in the payload frame, so transport depends only on the relative pose between the robots; global localisation reduces to coarse landmark-based estimation.
  • Relative pose is recovered through the kinematic chain of the shared grasp rather than odometry, making it immune to wheel slip and map drift; a right-invariant EKF on SE(3) estimates each grasp transform with χ² innovation gating at 50 Hz from 30 Hz wrist-camera detections.
  • A 14-DoF whole-body QP carries the closed-chain constraint as a soft cost weighted by the live estimator covariance, so coupling relaxes as the estimate degrades and the solver stays feasible mid-transport. Full stack running on hardware.
