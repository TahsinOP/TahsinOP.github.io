Visual SLAM for UAVs

A complete visual SLAM system, built from scratch for indoor UAV navigation.

  • SLAM
  • Perception

My contributions

  • Built a visual SLAM front end and back end from scratch with Open3D and OpenCV.
  • Implemented local and global loop closure with pose-graph optimisation, and bag-of-words relocalisation refined by ICP.
  • Validated handheld on a RealSense D455 with an Orin NX, and designed a tightly coupled VIO extension.

ORB-SLAM3 and RTAB-Map are hard to adapt, and no end-to-end 3D visual SLAM and navigation stack comparable to Nav2 existed for our UAV. So we built the front end and back end from scratch, using only Open3D and OpenCV.

Mapping

ICP registration, keyframe selection and incremental map building produce consistent dense maps of indoor spaces. Local and global loop closures feed a pose-graph optimiser that removes accumulated drift.

Localisation

Relocalisation uses bag-of-words place recognition, refined by ICP against stored keyframes, to recover the full 6-DoF camera pose.

Validation and next steps

The system was validated handheld on a RealSense D455 with an Orin NX. I also designed an extension to tightly coupled VIO, adding IMU preintegration factors to the pose graph.
