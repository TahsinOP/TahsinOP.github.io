Imitation Learning for Arm Manipulation

Two parallel eYSIP 2026 projects benchmarking policies on real hardware

  • Defined and led two parallel projects evaluating imitation learning policies on real hardware: a bimanual SO-101 setup (mentor, 3 interns) and a UR5 arm (co-mentor, 1 intern).
  • Benchmarked ACT and SmolVLA across bimanual pick and place, cube stacking, precision insertion and single-arm grasping, guiding dataset collection and end-to-end training; ran an ablation swapping an RGB wrist camera for a RealSense D405, modifying the ACT codebase for the extra modality.
  • Built a teleoperation and data pipeline for the UR5, a platform LeRobot does not support: Pika Sense teleoperation, joint and image capture to ROS bags, and conversion into LeRobot format — a learned counterpart to the classical GraspNet and MoveIt pipeline built the previous year.
