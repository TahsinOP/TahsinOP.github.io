Tahsin Khan

Senior Project Technical Assistant, ERTS Lab (e-Yantra), IIT Bombay

I'm a robotics engineer at the ERTS Lab, IIT Bombay, working on autonomy for ground and aerial robots. I hold a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from NITK Surathkal.

My recent work covers decentralised cooperative mobile manipulation, LiDAR and visual SLAM on UAVs, monocular autonomy for Ackermann vehicles, and remote robotics education platforms. Along the way I have worked on state estimation, motion planning, optimisation-based control and imitation learning for bimanual manipulation.

Decentralised Cooperative Mobile Manipulation

Two custom mobile manipulators carry one payload with onboard vision only: no mocap, no shared map.

  • Posed planning and control in the payload frame, so transport depends only on the relative pose between the two robots.
  • Recovered relative pose through the kinematic chain of the shared grasp, with an EKF on SE(3) running at 50 Hz on a Jetson Orin.
