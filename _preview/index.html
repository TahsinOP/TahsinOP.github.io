Currently working at the ERTS Lab (e-Yantra), IIT Bombay a robotics outreach programme where i develop theme stacks that students across India compete on through the e-Yantra Robotics Competition , currently developing Niti Vahan, an Ackermann vehicle that has to drive a scaled city from a single forward-facing camera. Previous year worked on Krishi Cobot, a greenhouse automation system where two industrial robots a mobile robot and a UR5 manipulator collaboratively doing tasks. The whole system was given remote acess through our platform all over India

My ongoing reseach work is towards cooperative non-holonomic mobile manipulation: Two mobile manipulators carrying a shared payload . Previous work involves visual SLAM (Mapping) written from scratch, 3D LiDAR SLAM flown on a Holybro UAV and lately, exploring learning for manipulation, navigation
